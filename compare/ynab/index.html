Cleer Money vs YNAB

Updated 8 July 2026 · Competitor details last checked 7 July 2026

TL;DR

YNAB is the gold standard for hands-on zero-based budgeting, where you assign every dollar a job before you spend. Cleer Money is the opposite philosophy: no budget to build or maintain, just clarity on where your money goes through wants versus needs. Choose YNAB if you want to actively control every dollar and will invest the time. Choose Cleer Money if budgeting has never stuck and you just want to see your spending clearly.

Where YNAB is stronger

A proven method with two decades behind it, a very large and active community, strong educational content, cross-platform apps and family sharing for up to six people on one subscription. For someone who wants to actively direct every dollar, it is hard to beat.

Where Cleer Money is different

No budgeting system to learn or maintain, priced in AUD, and built natively for Australian Open Banking. The focus is awareness rather than control. It is for people who have tried budgeting and bounced off it. If that sounds familiar, the money awareness method explains the idea in full.

Who should choose which

The short version

  • Choose YNAB if you are a committed active budgeter who wants structure and cross-platform control.
  • Choose Cleer Money if you are a budget-fatigued Australian who wants spending clarity without the upkeep.
