Cleer Money vs WeMoney

Updated 8 July 2026 · Competitor details last checked 7 July 2026

TL;DR

WeMoney is a free Australian app built around debt payoff, free credit scores and a social community, funded by connecting you to lending and product offers. Cleer Money is a paid, offers-free app focused purely on spending awareness. Choose WeMoney if you want free tracking, your credit score and debt tools. Choose Cleer Money if you want a focused, ad-free and offer-free way to understand your spending.

Where WeMoney is stronger

It is free, includes free credit score monitoring, has debt consolidation tools and a large community and connects a wide range of account types. If you want one dashboard for everything, including your credit score, WeMoney does far more than Cleer Money.

Where Cleer Money is different

No ads and no product offers, a single behavioural lens of wants versus needs rather than a broad dashboard, plus a subscription model that means the product is not monetised by referring you to lenders. You can read more about that single lens in the money awareness method.

Who should choose which

The short version

  • Choose WeMoney if you want free all-in-one tracking with a credit score and debt focus.
  • Choose Cleer Money if you want a paid, distraction-free tool aimed only at spending awareness.
