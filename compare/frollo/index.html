Cleer Money vs Frollo

Updated 8 July 2026 · Competitor details last checked 7 July 2026

TL;DR

Frollo is a free Australian personal finance app and a pioneer of the Consumer Data Right, offering budgets, goals, net worth and categorisation with no ads. Cleer Money is a paid app with one specific method: sorting spending into wants and needs. Choose Frollo if you want a free, broad Open Banking money manager. Choose Cleer Money if you want a simpler, behaviour-first approach without building budgets.

Where Frollo is stronger

It is free, is a genuine CDR pioneer with broad Australian bank coverage, has no ads and offers a fuller feature set including budgets, goals and net worth tracking. If you want a complete money manager at no cost, Frollo does more than Cleer Money.

Where Cleer Money is different

It deliberately drops budgets and net worth dashboards in favour of one behavioural question, want or need, which keeps it simple for people who find full personal finance apps overwhelming. The thinking behind that is set out in the money awareness method.

Who should choose which

The short version

  • Choose Frollo if you want a free, comprehensive Open Banking money manager.
  • Choose Cleer Money if you want a focused, budget-free awareness tool.
