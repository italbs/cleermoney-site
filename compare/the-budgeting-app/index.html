Cleer Money vs The Budgeting App

Updated 7 October 2026 · Competitor details last checked 7 October 2026

TL;DR

The Budgeting App is a manual-first spend tracker from New Zealand, with an optional Pro Sync plan that adds bank feeds and needs and wants sorting. Cleer Money is built the other way round: an Australian budgeting app where your bank connection and the want or need swipe are the core of the product, not an add-on. Both connect Australian banks through the Consumer Data Right. Choose The Budgeting App if you want to log spending by hand, use Android or track several currencies. Choose Cleer Money if you want your transactions to come in automatically and the bank-connected plan for less.

Where The Budgeting App is stronger

It is on Android as well as iPhone, works offline, supports multiple currencies and has a cheap manual-only Pro plan with a lifetime option. It also has more options for people who like to log by hand: budget rollover, a calendar view, savings and loan trackers, tags and an app lock. If you want to type in every purchase yourself, it is built for that.

Where Cleer Money is different

Cleer Money starts from your bank, not a blank ledger. Transactions arrive through Open Banking and you make one decision on each one: want or need. That decision drives everything else, from your spending targets and pace to the insights on what your wants are really costing you. The bank-connected plan costs $4 a month less, and want or need sorting is on every plan rather than locked to the top tier. The thinking behind it is set out in the money awareness method.

Who should choose which

The short version

  • Choose The Budgeting App if you prefer logging spending by hand, use Android or need several currencies.
  • Choose Cleer Money if you want your Australian bank transactions sorted into wants and needs with as little effort as possible.
