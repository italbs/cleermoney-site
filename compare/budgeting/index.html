Cleer Money vs budgeting and spreadsheets

Updated 8 July 2026 · by Cleer Money

TL;DR

Traditional budgeting and spreadsheets give you total control and cost little or nothing, but they rely on discipline and upkeep that many people cannot sustain. Cleer Money removes the upkeep and replaces it with a single habit: sorting each transaction into a want or a need. Choose a budget or spreadsheet if you like planning and will maintain it. Choose Cleer Money if plans keep falling apart and you want awareness instead.

Where budgeting and spreadsheets are stronger

Free or nearly free, completely flexible, fully private if kept offline and genuinely effective for people who maintain them. A well-kept budget will always give you tighter control than an awareness tool, because you are deciding the limits in advance.

Where Cleer Money is different

Almost nothing to maintain, automatic transaction import and a single behavioural habit instead of a full plan. It trades control for consistency. You can read the reasoning behind it in the money awareness method.

Key takeaway

Why this matters

Budgeting fails most people not because the maths is hard but because the upkeep is. Cleer Money is built for the people budgeting has already lost. If you have kept a spreadsheet going for years, keep going. If you have not, awareness may be the habit that finally sticks.
