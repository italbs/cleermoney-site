Cleer Money vs PocketSmith

Updated 8 July 2026 · Competitor details last checked 7 July 2026

TL;DR

PocketSmith is a power-user tool built around long-range cash flow forecasting, multi-currency support and deep customisation, with a learning curve to match. Cleer Money is a simple, single-purpose awareness app. Choose PocketSmith if you want to model your finances years ahead across many accounts. Choose Cleer Money if you want quick clarity on everyday spending without the complexity.

Where PocketSmith is stronger

Long-range calendar-based forecasting, multi-currency support, unlimited accounts on higher tiers, a free manual tier and very deep reporting and customisation for people who want to plan decades ahead. For serious planners, Cleer Money does not try to compete here.

Where Cleer Money is different

It does one thing simply. No forecasting engine, no rule building, no dashboards to configure. Just automatic import and a want or need decision. The case for that simplicity is in the money awareness method.

Who should choose which

The short version

  • Choose PocketSmith if you are a planner or power user who wants forecasting and control.
  • Choose Cleer Money if you want fast, low-effort spending awareness.
