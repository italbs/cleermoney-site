About Cleer Money

Cleer Money is an Australian iPhone app designed for people who want to get better with money without relying on complicated traditional budgets.

Users swipe each purchase as a Want or a Need, creating a simple habit that helps show where their money is actually going.

Cleer remembers how merchants are categorised so transactions become easier to sort over time, while users can also set weekly, fortnightly or monthly spending targets by category.

Cleer Money can securely connect to Australian bank accounts through Australia's Consumer Data Right. Access is read-only. Cleer Money cannot move, transfer or spend a user's money.

Cleer Money was built by founder Isaac Talbot in Maroochydore on Queensland's Sunshine Coast.

Founder

Isaac Talbot is a 26-year-old founder from Maroochydore on Queensland's Sunshine Coast. He built Cleer Money while working as Business Manager of a Sunshine Coast disability services business, after seeing how many people want to get on top of their money but find traditional budgets difficult to maintain.

Cleer was built around a different idea: before trying to restrict someone's spending, help them understand it.

Founder photography and interviews available on request.

A different approach to budgeting

Traditional budgets often ask people to decide how much they should spend before they fully understand how they already spend.

Cleer takes the opposite approach.

Users review their real purchases and swipe each one as a Want or a Need. Over time, this creates a clearer picture of their spending habits and helps them decide where they actually want to make changes.
