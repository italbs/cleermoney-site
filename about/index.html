About Cleer Money

An independent Australian money awareness app, built to help people see where their money goes without budgeting.

Who we are

The company behind the app

Why we built it

Budgeting loses most people

Most budgeting tools fail for the same reason: the upkeep. Planning every category, then recording and reconciling spending, is work that few people sustain. We wanted something that asks almost nothing of you and still changes how you see your money.

So we built Cleer Money around a single habit. Connect your Australian bank through the Consumer Data Right, then sort each transaction into a want or a need with a swipe. That is the whole idea, and you can read it in full in the money awareness method.

What we build

One tool, done simply

Cleer Money connects to Australian banks through Open Banking via our Consumer Data Right partner Fiskil, imports your transactions automatically and turns each one into a want or need decision. Insights then show your want and need split, weekly patterns and top merchants. There are no budgets, no spreadsheets and no product offers.

See how it compares with the other options in budgeting alternatives in Australia.

How we make money

Subscriptions and only subscriptions

Cleer Money is funded by subscriptions alone. We do not sell your data, show ads or refer you to lenders. Your banking data is used for one thing: showing you your own spending. Access is read-only, so the app can never move your money, and you can withdraw consent at any time. When consent ends, your CDR data is permanently deleted immediately. The detail is in our Privacy Policy and on our CDR Information page.
