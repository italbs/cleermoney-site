How it works

Connect, swipe, see what it adds up to

There are no categories to set up and no spreadsheet to keep up to date.

1

Bring your spending in

On Pro, connect your bank through Open Banking (CDR) via Fiskil. It's read-only, so we can't move your money. On the free plan, add purchases yourself or import a CSV.

2

Swipe each purchase

Every transaction shows up as a card. Right if you needed it, left if you wanted it. On Pro, categories fill themselves in and Smart Rules remember the merchants you see every week.

3

See what it adds up to

Your wants, your top merchants, how this week compares with last, and whether you're on pace for the month.

Features

Simple to use. Private by design.

Open banking connection

Connect major Australian banks through the Consumer Data Right, the open banking framework regulated by the ACCC. Transactions sync automatically, and you can withdraw consent at any time.

The Want/Need swipe

One quick decision per purchase. Reviewing each one as a Want or a Need builds awareness that a budget spreadsheet never will, and Smart Rules auto-categorise the merchants you see every week.

Insights that mean something

Your Want/Need split, weekly breakdowns, busiest spending day, top merchants, savings rate and a month-end forecast. Set spending targets and see whether you're on pace before the month gets away from you.

Privacy-first, by design

Your banking data is used for one thing: showing you your own spending. We never sell it, never use it for advertising, and never see your bank login. Delete your account and your data goes with it.
