How it works

Four steps to financial clarity

No complex setup. No manual entry. Connect once, then a few swipes a week keeps your whole money picture clear.

1

Connect

Link your Australian bank securely through Open Banking (CDR) via Fiskil. Access is read-only, so Cleer Money can never move your money.

2

Categorise

On Pro, transactions sync and categorise themselves automatically through your bank connection: groceries, transport, streaming and more. No spreadsheets, no data entry. On Lite, import a CSV to get the same swipe experience.

3

Swipe

Each transaction becomes a card. Swipe right for Need, left for Want. It takes seconds and slowly rewires how you see your spending.

4

Understand

Insights show your Want/Need split, weekly patterns, top merchants and month-end forecast: the numbers that actually change behaviour.

Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Open banking connection

Connect major Australian banks through the Consumer Data Right, the open banking framework regulated by the ACCC. Transactions sync automatically, and you can withdraw consent at any time.

The Want/Need swipe

The five-second habit at the heart of Cleer Money. Reviewing each purchase as a Want or a Need builds awareness that a budget spreadsheet never will, and Smart Rules auto-categorise the merchants you see every week.

Insights that mean something

Your Want/Need split, weekly breakdowns, busiest spending day, top merchants, savings rate and a month-end forecast. Set spending targets and see whether you're on pace before the month gets away from you.

Privacy-first, by design

Your banking data is used for one thing: showing you your own spending. We never sell it, never use it for advertising, and never see your bank login. Delete your account and your data goes with it.
